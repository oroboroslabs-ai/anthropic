FAQ

Straight answers.

Is $59.99 really one-time?

Yes. One payment covers the Chrome, Edge, and Firefox editions for life. There is no recurring charge.

What is the voice-to-voice system?

It unifies the model, your local system, and your phone into one conversation. You speak; the model answers; the reply comes back as voice or full text, so it feels like talking to a person rather than operating a chatbot.

Does it send full messages?

Yes. WhatsApp replies can go out as native voice notes or full text through your local bridge. The conversation is not limited to short commands.

Does it translate voice?

Yes. Universal voice translation runs through AILANG across 35 languages, with a strict human-to-entity pass and back to the language you chose.

Can I control my system from my phone?

Yes. Your phone is a voice surface for the harness. Ask for an action; the local system executes it and returns the result as voice or full text.

Can I add my AI to a group chat?

Yes. Invite Claude into the group thread. It follows the conversation, answers when called, and can send voice or full text without leaving the group.

Do I need the Oroboros harness?

Yes. The extensions are the browser surface for your local harness at 127.0.0.1:8792. The harness handles voice, translation, memory, and tools.

Which browsers are included?

Chrome, Edge, and Firefox. Chrome and Edge use the Chromium Manifest V3 build. Firefox uses its own Manifest V3 sidebar build.

Does my voice leave the machine?

The extension sends your voice to your own local harness. Your harness controls where model and translation calls go; the extension does not add a separate cloud account.

What happens after purchase?

You receive the three packages and your lifetime license. Keep the bundle; future updates for the three editions are included while the product is maintained.
