The Command Ladder — move where she commands

ATTEND · TOWARD · COME

One lead = attention (heads turn). Two = walk in that direction. Three = come to the point. The model's hands_command move(x, y) carries the verb — attend, toward, come — the visual swarm obeys the ladder.

FIND — THE SWARM'S SEARCH

hands_command type=find value="the phrase" — the hands hunt the phrase across the live page and bring the whole crawl to it. The search the Architect commands.

GRIP THE PAGE OBJECTS

Select links, buttons, inputs: click, type_text, scroll, press, observe (the element registry with rects) — the model selects links and objects on the page the same way you would.
